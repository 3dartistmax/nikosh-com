---
title: "A Small Archive of Almosts"
date: 2026-11-22
excerpt: "A simple way to keep unfinished fragments available without turning every possibility into an obligation."
---

Creative practice produces fragments: a color pair, half a sentence, a thumbnail that never became a painting. These pieces are easy to dismiss because they do not have a final form. A small archive gives them a place to wait.

Keep the system simple. Save the fragment with a short note about what caught your attention—shape, rhythm, contrast, or a question. A date or a descriptive filename is often enough; elaborate sorting can become another task to avoid.

Not every fragment needs to be finished. Some are useful only because they make a future choice easier. Keeping them lets the work remain available without turning every possibility into an obligation.
