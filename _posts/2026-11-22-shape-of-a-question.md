---
title: "The Shape of a Question"
date: 2026-11-22
excerpt: "A small, testable question can give a drawing or a paragraph direction without deciding its destination."
---

A blank page can feel like a demand for a finished idea. It is often easier to begin with a question small enough to test: What if the horizon sat higher? What if one sentence carried the whole paragraph? A question gives the work a direction without deciding its destination.

In a drawing, that might mean making three quick compositions before choosing one. In writing, it might mean describing an object before explaining what it means. The answer can change as the work develops; the question only needs to keep you moving.

A useful beginning is not always an inspired one. Sometimes it is simply a clear place to look.
