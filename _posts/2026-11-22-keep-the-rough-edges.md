---
title: "Keep the Rough Edges"
date: 2026-11-22
excerpt: "Let an early draft reveal its structure before polishing away the questions it still needs to answer."
---

Early drafts make decisions visible. A thumbnail shows where the composition is leaning before details make it look inevitable; a rough paragraph exposes its central thought before polish smooths the transitions.

Revision matters, but polishing too soon can hide what still needs to be asked. Leave a few marks or sentences unresolved while the larger structure is changing. Then decide which roughness adds energy and which only creates confusion.

The goal is not to preserve every first impulse. It is to give the work enough time to reveal what deserves keeping. A finished piece can be clear without pretending that clarity was present from the start.
