# About Me

Well, what should I write about myself?

I am an ordinary person who wishes to share with readers my thoughts and experiences on contemporary, philosophical, spiritual, and other subjects. These reflections are shaped by what I have read, seen, heard, experienced, contemplated, and learned from others. Alongside articles, I will also share my paintings and musical compositions here.

And why this personal introduction in my own words?

I once came across a thought that stayed with me:

> "Every day, in cremation grounds, hundreds of people's stories, works of art, and dreams disappear forever into smoke and ashes without ever being written down, seen, or heard."

Perhaps it is the unease that comes from realizing that one day, after having been born, I too will vanish as though I had never existed. That may be what inspires me to leave behind a small trace of my existence for others to see.

Some may ask: in this age of computers and artificial intelligence, why write at all? Why paint?

The answer is simple: human beings experience life; computers do not. Have you ever heard of a computer dancing in the joy of love, suffering the pain of heartbreak, being uplifted by the fragrance of a flower, or struggling with hunger and thirst?

If any of my words or creations have a positive impact on someone's life, I will consider myself fortunate. If I unintentionally hurt anyone's feelings, I sincerely apologize. I warmly welcome all suggestions, thoughts, and feedback.

If you would like to get in touch, please feel free to contact me at **contact@nikosh.com.np**.

I will continue to revise, expand, and update this page over time as life unfolds and circumstances change.

**Note:** All articles, artworks, and other creative works shared on this website are my original creations. While I occasionally use Artificial Intelligence to assist with grammar, clarity, and language refinement, the ideas, perspectives, experiences, and creative expression remain entirely my own.
